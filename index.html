openSUSE Membership Application

Apply to become an openSUSE Member. Membership recognises sustained contribution to the project and its community. Complete your openSUSE wiki profile first — membership officials will review it alongside this application.

Applications are sent to membership-officials@lists.opensuse.org. Read the membership guidelines before you apply. Fields marked * are required before the application can be sent.
